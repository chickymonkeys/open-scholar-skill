# Descriptive

Routed to **R1**. Principles that bite hardest: **P3**, **P6**, **P7**.

A descriptive paper is not a failed causal paper. The question is whether the facts are measured
well, representative of the population claimed, and framed without causal or welfare overreach.

Prefix findings `DESC-`.

## Check 1 — Data quality and provenance

**Tests** whether source quality, coverage, missingness, definition changes over time, collection
incentives and known limitations are documented.
**Violation**: a central fact rests on data whose coverage, quality or construction is insufficiently
documented.
**Evidence required**: the data section, appendix or table note — or the missing quality discussion.

## Check 2 — Measurement validity

**Tests** whether the variables measure the concept claimed, and whether proxies, indices,
classifications and harmonisation choices are validated.
**Violation**: a proxy or constructed measure is treated as the underlying concept with no validation,
sensitivity or caveat.
**Evidence required**: the variable definition and the validation evidence, or its absence.

## Check 3 — Representativeness and weighting

**Tests** whether the sample represents the population or domain claimed, and whether weights or
coverage corrections are justified.
**Violation**: the paper generalises from a selected sample, platform, region, period or
administrative subset with no representativeness evidence and no caveat.
**Evidence required**: the sampling frame, coverage statement, weighting rule, or the claim language.

## Check 4 — Honest framing

**Tests** whether patterns are framed as descriptive rather than causal, normative or welfare claims.
**Violation**: correlations or trends carry causal, mechanism or policy-effect claims with no design
behind them.
**Evidence required**: the descriptive result and the overclaiming language.

## Invariants

- Do not ask a descriptive paper to become a causal paper. Flag only causal or policy language the
  paper actually uses.
- Measurement error and selection are findings when they change the interpretation of the fact being
  claimed.
- A transparent limitation can satisfy the framing standard even where the limitation remains.
