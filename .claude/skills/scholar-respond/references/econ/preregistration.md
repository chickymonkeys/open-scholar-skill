# Preregistration and Pre-Analysis Plans

Routed to **R1**. Principle that bites hardest: **P5**.

This dimension adds one thing no other reference has: a **time-ordering** evidence requirement. It
asks whether evidence presented as confirmatory was planned before the data were seen — a question
transparency cannot answer, because a fully disclosed analysis can still be a post-hoc one.

Prefix findings `PAP-`.

## Check 1 — Registration status

**Tests** whether an experiment, RCT or registered observational design says where and when it was
registered.
**Violation**: the paper invokes pre-specification, confirmatory testing or experimental credibility
and gives no registry, no date and no registration status.
**Evidence required**: the registration statement, plan section or footnote — or its absence.

## Check 2 — Outcomes and specifications against the plan

**Tests** whether primary outcomes, arms, hypotheses, sample rules and specifications match the plan.
**Violation**: central outcomes or specifications differ from the plan with no exploratory label and
no explanation.
**Evidence required**: the plan's description and the analysis table or text that departs from it.

## Check 3 — Deviations and amendments

**Tests** whether deviations, amendments, timing changes, attrition surprises and data constraints are
disclosed.
**Violation**: deviations are visible from the paper's own text but not disclosed as deviations; or
post-treatment amendments are not distinguished from pre-treatment plans.
**Evidence required**: the amendment or deviation statement, or the sections that conflict.

## Check 4 — Confirmatory against exploratory framing

**Tests** whether exploratory heterogeneity, mechanism, secondary-outcome and subgroup analyses are
labelled honestly.
**Violation**: exploratory results are written as pre-specified confirmatory tests with no
registration behind them.
**Evidence required**: the claim language and the missing or mismatched plan support.

## What the field tunes

Field experiments in economics are expected to be registered before submission — the AEA RCT Registry
for AEA journals. Registration norms bind much less on legacy observational work. Name the norm that
applies here before rating severity.

## Invariants

- Do not require preregistration from theory, historical descriptive work or legacy observational
  studies unless the paper claims pre-specification or confirmatory status.
- A missing plan is more severe for an experiment and less severe for openly exploratory
  observational work.
- Where the paper discloses a deviation honestly, the deviation itself is not a finding unless it
  undermines a stated claim.
